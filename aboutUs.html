Who We Are

Papa Bear Gifts is a family-owned business based in Maryland that sells used (primarily women's clothing) items online. Both storefronts have been operational since 2022!

What Do We Sell?

Papa Bear Gifts usually sells used casual clothing items for women, typically in Small through Medium sizes.

Occasionally, we list other (typically used but sometimes New With Tags (NWT)) items such as accessories, beauty/skincare products (including Korean brands), technology parts, kitchen items, room decor, books, dance apparel, and figure skating apparel.

With the exception of NWT items, all of the clothes in our inventory have been washed. All items are stored in a smoke-free and pet-free environment.

Our "Locations"

We're proud to say that we sell in two well-known "locations." Papa Bear Gifts currently sells on the ecommerce platforms Poshmark and eBay. While both stores sell almost the exact same amount of items, Poshmark showcases every item in our inventory with items priced as low as $3. Items that are $5 or more can be found on eBay.

Whether which platform is best to purchase from simply depends on your preferences. However, if you intend to purchase multiple items or plan to purchase a higher-value item, eBay may be the better choice because the shipping rates are comparably lower (more details about shipping can be found on our Shipping page within our website's navigation).

While each "location" or "branch" has its own username, both stores are run by Papa Bear Gifts. The Poshmark store can be found under the username "nskates" and the eBay store can be found under the username "nshop-official."

Links to both storefronts can be found in our website's homepage (which can be accessed by clicking the "Home" link in the navigation menu)!
